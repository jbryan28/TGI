Certification questions

Know the standard before you begin.

What does CDZA™ stand for?+

Compression → Displacement → Zone → Authorization. It is the structural sequence at the foundation of the Daily Zone Command™ methodology.

Must the 20 trades be profitable?+

No. Certification evaluates process, not short-term variance. A slightly negative result may still qualify when all twenty trades meet the full Grade-A execution standard.

What happens after a non-compliant trade?+

The trade does not count and the sequence resets. The standard requires twenty consecutive examples of fully compliant professional behavior.

Is this a regulatory or investment-adviser credential?+

No. CDZA™ is an institute-issued execution standard within the Trader Growth Institute educational framework. It is not a securities license, regulatory qualification, or authorization to provide personalized financial advice.
