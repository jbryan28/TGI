Plain-language commitment

We collect only what is needed to operate and improve TraderGrowth.com. The Weekly Capital Review is stored locally in your browser and is not transmitted to Trader Growth LLC.

Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Trader Growth LLC ("Trader Growth," "TGI," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you visit TraderGrowth.com, use our educational resources, join a mailing list, purchase a product, or otherwise interact with us.

Information we collect

Information you provide

We may receive your name, email address, billing details, account information, survey responses, support messages, and other information you choose to provide. Payment-card information is generally processed by the applicable payment provider rather than stored directly by TGI.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting and security providers may record technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, pages viewed, approximate location, timestamps, and diagnostic or security events.

Local browser data

The Weekly Capital Review uses browser local storage so your entries persist on your device. Those entries are not sent to TGI unless you independently choose to export and share them. Clearing browser storage may permanently remove them.

How we use information

  • Provide, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the website and educational products.
  • Process transactions and administer accounts, memberships, or course access.
  • Deliver requested communications and respond to support inquiries.
  • Measure site performance and improve content, products, and user experience.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, security threats, or violations of our terms.
  • Meet legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell personal information for money. If our practices materially change, we will update this policy before making the new use.

When information is shared

We may disclose limited information to service providers that help operate hosting, analytics, email delivery, payments, course access, customer support, and security. These providers may process information only for the services they perform for us, subject to their terms and applicable law.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate misuse, enforce agreements, or complete a merger, financing, reorganization, or sale of business assets.

Third-party websites and platforms have their own privacy practices. Review those policies before submitting information to them.

Your choices and rights

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails through the link in the message. You can control cookies and local storage through your browser, although disabling them may affect site functionality.

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of certain processing. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request, and lawful exceptions may apply.

Security, retention, and international use

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including legal, security, accounting, and dispute-resolution needs. Visitors outside the United States understand that information may be processed in the United States or other countries where service providers operate.

Children

TraderGrowth.com is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website.

Policy changes

We may revise this policy as our services or legal obligations change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be presented through an appropriate notice.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to jay@tradergrowth.com.
