What are Daily Zones?

Daily Zones are price areas on the daily chart where buyers or sellers previously moved price strongly. Jay marks these areas so you can study what happens when price returns. A zone is an area to watch, not a guaranteed turning point.

What is included in the $99/month membership?

Jay’s published Daily Zones, chart commentary, market scenarios, the Daily Zone Command™ course, and the book PDF. You also get weekly market news, selected trade reviews, 43 market pages, and the historical archive.

Are the DZC course and book included?

Yes. The Daily Zone Command™ course and PDF edition of the book are included in the $99/month membership. Print and Kindle editions, certification, and one-to-one mentoring are separate purchases.

What happens after I join?

Complete enrollment through Thinkific, then sign in to the member portal. Begin with Start Here, choose a market, and read its latest dated chart review.

How often is the Desk updated?

Weekly market news is published in News & Releases. Jay adds zones, commentary, and scenarios as conditions develop. Not every market gets a new review each day. Check each review’s date and status; an older chart is historical, and a market marked “Not reviewed” has no current assessment.

Will I get instructions to buy or sell?

No. Jay shares charts and explains what he is watching. You decide whether to trade, how much to risk, and when to exit. Membership does not include live trade alerts or promise daily trades.

Can I use the Desk on my phone?

Yes. Sign in to the Thinkific member portal using a mobile or desktop browser.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage your subscription in your Thinkific account’s billing settings. Cancel before your next renewal to stop future monthly charges. Access continues through the paid billing period. Review the refund and access terms shown at checkout.
