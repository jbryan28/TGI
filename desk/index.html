Is this a signal service?

No. Daily Zones and scenarios are educational observations, not instructions to buy or sell. You remain responsible for your own analysis, position sizing, risk, and decisions.

What happens after I join?

Complete enrollment through Thinkific, then access the member portal. Begin with Start Here and the DZC Operating Rules. Open Current Desk to scan the full market board, read News & Releases, and select an instrument’s permanent page.

Which markets are covered?

The current portal contains 43 instrument pages: 33 forex pairs, four indices (DXY, NAS100, US30, and SPX), four commodities (gold, silver, WTI, and Brent), and two cryptocurrencies (Bitcoin and XRP). Instruments on the board are not necessarily currently reviewed or presenting a setup.

How often is the Desk updated?

Weekly news announcements sit in News & Releases. Market outlooks, Daily Zones, structural levels, and scenarios are published as conditions develop. Check each entry’s date and current status: an instrument may be Not reviewed, have No setup, or be Watching. Daily Zones refers to the methodology; it does not guarantee a new review of all 43 instruments every day. Membership does not promise continuous monitoring, instant alerts, or daily trades.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage your subscription in your Thinkific account’s billing settings. Cancel before your next renewal to stop future monthly charges. Access continues through the paid billing period. Cancellation does not itself refund a payment; review the refund and access terms shown at checkout.

Does membership include the book, a course, or certification?

This offer is for the TGI Operating Desk. The Daily Zone Command book, any standalone course, CDZA certification, and one-to-one mentorship are separate and are not included in this membership.

Can I use the Desk on my phone?

You can access the Thinkific-hosted member portal through a desktop or mobile browser, using the same permanent instrument pages. A branded TGI app is separate from the browser access included here.
