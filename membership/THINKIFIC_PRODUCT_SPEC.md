# Trader Growth Institute Membership — Thinkific Product Specification

This file is the implementation source of truth for the paid Trader Growth Institute Membership. It supersedes the legacy paid product named **TGI Intelligence Briefing** and every June 28, 2026 availability claim.

## Product architecture

- Public sales route: `https://www.tradergrowth.com/membership/`
- Legacy book route: `https://www.tradergrowth.com/member/` redirects to `/membership/`
- Free publication: **TGI Intelligence Briefing**
- Paid product: **Trader Growth Institute Membership**
- Fixed curriculum: **Capital Operator Core**
- Recurring service: **TGI Operating Desk**
- Community: **Capital Operator Room**
- Separate standard: **CDZA™ Certification**

Thinkific membership contents:

1. Capital Operator Core
2. TGI Operating Desk
3. Capital Operator Resource Vault
4. Capital Operator Room
5. Monthly Operator Clinic Archive

The product is educational. It is not a signal service, copy-trading room, personalized advisory relationship, profit promise, or one-to-one mentorship program.

## Operating Desk calendar

- Sunday, 4:00 p.m. ET: Weekly Capital Brief
- Monday–Friday, 7:30 a.m. ET: Daily Desk Status
- Friday, 5:00 p.m. ET: Weekly Capital Review
- First Saturday monthly, 11:00 a.m. ET: 60-minute Operator Clinic, recorded

A Daily Desk Status may state **No material change — preserve capital**. Every scenario must define location, institutional evidence, authorization, invalidation, risk, and no-trade conditions. Do not publish isolated entries, stops, targets, or instructions to act immediately.

## Pricing and billing

Launch plan:

- $99 per month
- No free trial
- No setup fee
- No coupons
- No lifetime plan
- No course-only plan

Annual plan:

- $990 per year
- Disabled during the initial verification cohort
- Enabled only after four consecutive Weekly Capital Briefs, twenty weekday Desk Status posts, four Weekly Capital Reviews, and one Operator Clinic are delivered on time
- Annual renewal reminder sent 14 days before billing

Billing rules:

- Subscription automatically renews until cancelled
- Seven-day refund window on the first payment only
- Renewals are nonrefundable
- No prorated refunds
- Cancellation remains effective through the paid billing period
- Applicable taxes are added at checkout
- Failed renewal ends access on the renewal date
- Amount, frequency, renewal, refund, and cancellation terms must be displayed immediately before purchase

## Access rules

- Members must be 18 or older.
- Accounts are personal and nontransferable.
- Core, desk, community, recordings, and archives require an active subscription.
- There is no lifetime access after cancellation.
- Designated worksheets may be downloaded; videos and desk briefings may not be redistributed.
- No account sharing, reposting, resale, or screen recording.
- No personalized financial advice or individualized trade approval.
- No one-to-one mentorship is included.
- Community questions receive a response within two business days.
- Market briefings remain in the current archive for 90 days and are labeled historical afterward.
- Simulation is accepted and encouraged for all implementation exercises.
- P&L does not establish course completion or competence.

## CDZA™ separation

- Public journal, checklist, and tracking tools remain available at `/cdza/`.
- The membership includes CDZA readiness education, not certification approval.
- Level I requires 20 consecutive Grade-A compliant trades.
- Simulation or demo execution is acceptable for Level I because the standard evaluates protocol fidelity rather than account size or profit.
- Any noncompliant trade resets the sequence.
- Formal certification verification is not sold until the audit rubric, evidence standards, reviewer capacity, privacy procedures, appeals, resubmission, and revocation rules exist.
