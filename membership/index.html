Fit before enrollment

Built for serious traders. Wrong for shortcut seekers.

Membership is designed for traders who:

  • Already understand basic chart mechanics
  • Want a repeatable operating process
  • Accept risk limits and documentation
  • Prefer conditional scenarios over predictions

Membership is not designed for traders who:

  • Want copy trades or entry alerts
  • Expect guaranteed profitability
  • Refuse structured risk limits
  • Measure quality by excitement or trade volume
