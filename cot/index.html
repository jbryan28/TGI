      Methodology

      Positioning is evidence. Not permission.

      01

      Read the participant

      Financial contracts default to Leveraged Funds. Physical commodities default to Managed Money. Change the participant control to inspect the other side of the market.

      02

      Read the reporting lag

      The report normally reflects positions held at Tuesday’s close and is released Friday. It is strategic context, not real-time order flow.

      03

      Read price authority

      Combine positioning with macro regime, Daily Zones, displacement, invalidation, and risk governance before considering capital deployment.
